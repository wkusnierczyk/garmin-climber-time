Climber Time is a watch face with a climbing wall: the time on the left, a wall of holds on the right, and a tiny climber who marks the seconds, one climb per minute.

Every 15 minutes the climber works up through grades 4 to 8. Each grade's route is climbed three times: by a beginner, who falls onto the rope and never reaches the top; by an intermediate, who may fall once or miss a jump; and by an expert, who sends it. Each new grade brings a new route in a new colour. At the end of every minute the climber is lowered off on the rope, ready for the next attempt. The grade and the climber's level (B, I or E) are shown under the time.

On AMOLED screens, in always-on mode the face shows the time, under a fine checkerboard that shifts every minute, and the route's holds as small dimmed dots that move slightly every minute, to protect the screen. On MIP screens (such as the fēnix 7, the Enduro 3 and the Forerunner 955) the wall has no panel, and the whole face stays on, updated once a minute when the wrist is down.

The face has no settings: it is drawn as designed.

Note: watch faces redraw at most once per second, so the climber moves in small steps rather than smoothly.
